'''Nephis''' is a descendant of the [[Legacy Clans#Immortal Flame|Immortal Flame Clan]]<ref name=":27">Chapter 27 Measure Of Power</ref>, and one of the important characters in Shadow Slave.

She is the Star of Ruin, the last daughter of the Immortal Flame clan, and the inheritor of the fire.<ref name=":1472" />
